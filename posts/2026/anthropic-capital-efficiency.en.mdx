On that chart, the cut is private companies with a $10B+ valuation and $100M+ in publicly reported revenue. OpenAI is listed at $1.25T on $50B of revenue, a 25x. Anthropic is listed at $965B on $81B of revenue, an 11.9x. Several smaller names print much higher multiples. xAI is marked 500x; that deal price includes X, and the revenue is AI only. Mercor shows 6.7x, on gross revenue. Multiples built off $100M+ or $400M+ revenue tags are upper bounds.

The point of the chart is not a league table. Biggest is not the same as expensive.

## The GAAP loss is not operating spend

At first glance, people see revenue growing very fast and a GAAP loss, and they assume costs are very high.

A substantial part of that loss is liability from past fundraising, debt-to-equity conversions, and equity appreciation. That is not the same as its real operating spend.

I wouldn't just look at how much it is still losing.

## What is actually worth watching

What is worth watching is how much cash it is putting in, relative to current revenue, ARR, and the growth rate.

From a startup point of view, I think this kind of capital-efficient growth is unlike anything I've seen.

The real question should be: to create one more dollar of new intelligence, revenue, and future earnings, how much does it actually need to burn?

That is the analysis. The NVIDIA talking point is a separate conversation. It does not change this one.
