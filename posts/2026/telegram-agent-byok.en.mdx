Leak watchers found a hidden "Agent" feature in Telegram Beta for macOS (build 283365). You paste your own API key from ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, or OpenRouter, then chat with `@agent`.

Your key. Your bill. Paid to OpenAI or Anthropic — not Telegram.

That is the whole product, and that is the whole point.

Original post: [https://x.com/Howard_Pengg/status/2113956115868418126](https://x.com/Howard_Pengg/status/2113956115868418126)

## What shipped (quietly)

This is not an official launch. The feature is off by default. It may change before release. Telegram has not announced it.

What the beta shows is simple: bring your own key (BYOK), pick one of five providers — OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, xAI, OpenRouter — and talk to an agent inside the chat app you already open every day.

No Telegram-hosted model in the middle of that path. No Telegram meter on the inference. The money leaves the chat and lands with the model company.

## Better UX, not a business model

My take: this looks like a move made out of necessity.

Telegram still hasn't found a way to make money from AI. The $300M Grok distribution deal announced in May 2025 was never confirmed as signed — Musk said the same day that no deal had been signed. Telegram's own AI Editor, which did ship, runs on Cocoon.

So `@agent` is better UX. A nice add. Not a game changer for Telegram's P&L.

That is not a dunk. It is a read of the product as shipped: Telegram is optimizing for where agents live, not for who collects the API bill.

## The bigger signal

AI is moving into the chat app people already open every day.

Agents belong in Telegram. Distribution is the scarce part. The model layer is already crowded and already monetized. The chat layer is where users, bots, payments, and group context already sit.

Telegram just isn't the one getting paid for the agent call yet.

If that stays true, `@agent` is still strategically correct for users and for anyone building on top of Telegram. It just doesn't answer how Telegram itself captures AI revenue. Those are two different problems. This beta only solves the first one.

## What I'm watching

- Whether BYOK stays the default path, or Telegram later wraps a hosted model and takes a cut.
- Whether `@agent` stays a side feature or becomes the default way bots and users call models inside Telegram.
- Whether "agents in the chat you already use" becomes the distribution pattern everyone else copies — because that pattern does not require Telegram to win the model race.

Not announced. Off by default. May change before release. The signal is still clear: agents are migrating into Telegram, and Telegram is letting the model vendors keep the meter.
